where connections become communities

iris is a member aggregation and connection platform which enables organizations to better sort their data and use it for outreach and fundraising purposes.

What Iris does
understand and activate your network
Aggregate Member Data
Connect your existing database or upload a roster. Iris will organize it automatically.
Enrich Profiles with Live Data
Current information and professional context will be synced with your existing data - reflecting where members are today.
Visualize and Explore Connections
See your network how you'd like it. Choose how you filter your data and assign custom tags.
Network Better
See success with structured outreach, powered by AI, both for organizations and members.
